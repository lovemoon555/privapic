# Javelin Duel – Aim, Throw, Beat the Boss
  
  **Javelin Duel** is a cartoon dusk-lit throwing duel for one or two players. Take turns hurling
  javelins, axes and bombs at your rival — wind, pillars and weather all bend the shot, and where you
  hit decides the damage: head x1.5, body x1.0, legs x0.7. Clear your opponent's health first to win
  the duel.
  
  ## Features
  
  - 15-level campaign across 3 chapters, with a boss at the end of each
  - Quick match vs AI (Easy / Normal / Hard) or local 2-player on one device
  - Six fighters, each with a unique once-per-match special move
  - Three weapons: Javelin, Axe and Bomb
  - Part-based hits — aim for the head for extra damage
  - Wind, moving stone pillars and weather that change every shot
  - Daily missions and 9 achievements
  - Game Center leaderboard and achievements
  - Music, sound and haptics, each with its own switch
  - English and Simplified Chinese
  - No ads. No in-app purchases. Works fully offline.

  ## Privacy Policy
  
  Javelin Duel takes your privacy seriously and runs entirely on your device.
  
  - **No data collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
  - **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.**
  - **Local storage only:** Game progress (coins, campaign stars, settings, daily missions,
  achievements) is stored exclusively on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
  - **Game Center (optional):** If you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings, the game submits
  your total campaign stars to a leaderboard and reports achievements through Apple's Game Center
  service. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we never receive or store your
  Game Center data. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.
  - **Children:** The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat and no data collection.
  
  If a future version adds online features or third-party services, this policy will be updated first.
  
  ## FAQ
  
  **Q: How do I win a duel?**
  A: Each throw's damage depends on where it lands — head x1.5, body x1.0, legs x0.7. Read the wind,
  pick your angle and power, and empty your opponent's health bar first.
  
  **Q: Do I need an account or internet connection?**
  A: No. Javelin Duel works completely offline with no sign-in needed. Game Center is optional and
  only used if you are already signed in via iOS Settings.
  
  **Q: What are the special moves?**
  A: Each of the six fighters (Fox, Bear, Rabbit, Duck, Wolf, Monkey) has its own style and a
  once-per-match special move.
  
  **Q: Is any of my data uploaded to the cloud?**
  A: No. All game progress is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded or tracked.
